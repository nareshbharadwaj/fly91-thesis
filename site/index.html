The storm is cost, not competition: fuel up 67% (ATF +46%), with a weak rupee on top. The leader is passing it on as higher fares and flat capacity, and the most price-sensitive flyers, often regional and first-time, are the ones being priced out.

Airline obituaries

More than 20 Indian airlines have shut down since 1994 . Among the regional ones, the causes rhyme:

Air PegasusShut within about a yearLease defaults to its lessor
Air Costa2013 – 2017"Failed to induct aircraft" fast enough
Air OdishaShut 2020UDAN licences cancelled: flights below 70% regularity
Deccan Charters · ZexusShut 2020Sub-scale regional operations

Business Today, "Runway to Nowhere"; government data in Parliament

Regional airlines die of three things: scale, lessors and distribution. The 40-aircraft order answers scale. Distribution is the job this role exists to do.

8Arrival

Requesting information

Public data runs out here. These are the numbers that would turn the three experiments from hypotheses into plans, and the conversation I'd most like to have.

REQ 01
What share of bookings come direct (web, app, WhatsApp) vs OTAs and agents?
REQ 02
Ancillary revenue per passenger, and attach rates for SmartBag91 and Smart Ticket?
REQ 03
What share are first-time flyers, how often do they come back, and what does acquiring one cost by city?
REQ 04
How far apart are load factors across routes: UDAN vs others, islands vs metros?
REQ 05
Is there corporate or SME demand for a prepaid, flexible pass?
REQ 06
What does "a truly differentiated digital e-commerce business" mean to you: airline retail, a travel marketplace, or beyond travel?
